Solo project · Accessibility & AI

Noor AI — voice companion for the visually impaired

"Light for independence." An offline-first voice assistant built to give visually impaired users more independence in daily life — without depending on a constant internet connection.

Problem

Visually impaired users often depend on others for routine tasks — medication timing, indoor navigation, reaching help in an emergency.

Solution

A voice-first assistant handling SOS alerts, medicine and hydration reminders, indoor guidance, and time/date assistance, built to keep working without a constant connection.

Technology

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Node.js, Web Speech API.

Impact

Aligned with three UN Sustainable Development Goals: Quality Education, Reduced Inequalities, and Good Health & Well-being.

  • Voice interaction
  • Emergency SOS
  • Medicine reminders
  • Indoor guidance

Internship project · Indian Servers AI & Cyber Security

TrigVerse — a 3D trigonometry learning lab

An interactive 3D environment that turns abstract trigonometry into something you can see and manipulate, with an AI mentor guiding the way.

Problem

Trigonometry is usually taught through static diagrams and formulas, which makes it hard for students to build real intuition.

Solution

A 3D holographic unit circle with real-time sine/cosine visualization, dynamic formulas, and AI-powered explanations layered on top.

Technology

Next.js, React, React Three Fiber, Framer Motion, Google Gemini API.

Impact

Integrated Gemini AI for real-time, gamified mentorship — increasing learner engagement through adaptive feedback.

  • 3D unit circle
  • Gamified learning
  • AI explanations

Solo project · AI & Data

Multilingual News Hub

A real-time news translator built for readers who are more comfortable in Hindi, Telugu, or Urdu than English.

Problem

Most real-time news aggregation tools only serve English-language readers, leaving out large multilingual audiences.

Solution

An app that collects news via RSS feeds and translates full articles into Hindi, Telugu, Urdu, and English on demand.

Technology

Python, Streamlit, Feedparser, BeautifulSoup, SQLite, Deep Translator.

Impact

Makes real-time global news genuinely accessible across four languages from a single interface.

  • Real-time RSS
  • 4 languages
  • Full-article translation
